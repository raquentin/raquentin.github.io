## Overview

The apple green/oolong blend is quite nice. It claims to include white hibiscus, green tea, lemongrass, pineapple, apple, sweet blackberry leaves, natural flavors, and marigolds. The main flavor in this tea is one of green apple at the front. It's a bit oversweet considering this tea is advertised as "tart". Over around 200ms, the appleness fades linearly, increasingly revealing a decent green tea flavor.

If I were to brew again, I would likely use more leaf, as the flavor subsides quite quickly and is never as tart as I'd prefer.

**Score: 65**


## Hot Brew

I brewed this with 36mL of tea for 1L of water in 210 seconds. **Subscore: 61**
