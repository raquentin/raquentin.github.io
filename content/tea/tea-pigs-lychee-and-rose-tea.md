## Overview

This is a respectable floral tisane. It claims to include apple pieces, white hibiscus, hibiscus, rose lychee flavoring, turmeric, citiric acid, red rose petals, and natural lychee flavoring. The two lychee flavorings is a bit curious, it kind of implies the first lychee flavoring is not natural. Interesting. That aside, the tea has a nice, subdued yet lasting lychee flavor. I'm not sure what to make of the "rose" part as I don't really know what a rose tastes like.

**Score: 58**

I brewed one bag (2.5g) of tea cold for 400mL of water in 7 minutes.
